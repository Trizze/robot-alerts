How it works

One robot, shared progress across participating Twitch channels.

Earn points

Tier 1 subscriptions earn 1 point, Tier 2 earns 2, Tier 3 earns 5. Every 500 bits earns 1 point. Gifted subs credit the gifter by tier. Anonymous gifts and gift recipients earn no points. Resubs award points when a resub alert is shared.

Unlock your look

Tiers start at 10, 100, 250, 500 and 1,000 points. Each tier entry grants a random unowned pattern. In Tiers 1–4, additional patterns are spread evenly between your current tier threshold and the next. Tier 5 grants another pattern every 100 points, until you own them all.

Multiple channels, same you.

Points and your robot design stick with you even across streams that are connected to Robot Alerts.

Make it yours

Choose an earned skin, adjust its size, and put an emoji or Twitch emote on the chest display. Locked skins can still be previewed.
