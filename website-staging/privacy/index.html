Privacy

YOUR DATA

Small footprint.

Robot Alerts stores your Twitch user ID, display name, selected pattern, pattern size and chest display choice. It also keeps point-award records containing your Twitch ID, the participating channel ID, event type, points and time. Event IDs are kept for seven days to prevent duplicates. Session records contain a hash and an expiry of up to 30 days. Streamer credentials are encrypted and removed when the channel disconnects. Connected channel names are shown in the public Streamers directory. Streamer accounts also store overlay resolution, theme and alert-box design preferences.

The service does not store email addresses, IP addresses, chat messages or browser details. You can manage deletion below after signing in. Streamers can also disconnect their channel and remove its point records.
