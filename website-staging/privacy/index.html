Privacy

Your data, kept small and under your control.

What we store

Your robot
Twitch ID, display name, points, unlocked/seen patterns, equipped skin, size and chest display.
Your contributions
Viewer and channel IDs, event type, points and timestamp. No message text or payment details.
Streamer setup
Connected channel, public categories, event switches, resolution and alert design.

Retention & security

Event IDs
7 days, to prevent duplicates.
Sign-in sessions
Hashed, expire within 30 days.
Twitch credentials
Encrypted; removed when disconnected.
Unlock notices
Structured counts, tier and event type only; removed after delivery or within 7 days. Bot credentials are encrypted.
Anonymous gifts
One aggregate sub count per channel; no hidden donor identity is available or stored.
Never collected
Emails, IPs, chat messages or browser fingerprints.

Your controls

Deleting viewer data removes points and appearance across channels. Disconnecting a channel deletes its contributions and recalculates affected totals.

Manage account data

Deletion is permanent. Review what will be removed before confirming.

Public information

Connected streamers appear in the directory with their public Twitch picture and category. Your robot is shown when an alert is triggered.
